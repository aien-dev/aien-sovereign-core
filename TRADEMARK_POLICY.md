# Trademark & Brand Guidelines

This document clarifies usage policies regarding project names and brand references within the AIEN ecosystem.

## Project Names and Descriptive Use

Names such as "AIEN", "Atlas", and "SparkOS" are descriptive names identifying this open-source research project and its software artifacts. The maintainers do not hold registered trademarks for these names and make no representation of registered trademark ownership.

## Apache 2.0 Trademark Terms

> "This License does not grant permission to use the trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of the Licensor, except as required for reasonable and customary use in describing the origin of the Work and reproducing the content of the NOTICE file."

## Permitted Uses

You may freely:
1. State accurately that your software is based on, compatible with, or derived from AIEN open-source code.
2. Link to official AIEN repositories and documentation.
3. Distribute unmodified copies of the software with the accompanying NOTICE and LICENSE files.

## Restrictions

To avoid confusion among users and operators:
1. Do not name or market a product or service in a way that falsely implies official endorsement, certification, or sponsorship by Drake Stapleton or the AIEN core project.
2. Do not state or imply that modified downstream forks represent the official upstream AIEN distribution.

## Third-Party Marks

All third-party trademarks, service marks, trade names, and product names (including Modular, MAX, Mojo, NVIDIA, Grace Blackwell, and Hugging Face) belong to their respective owners. Their mention in this repository is purely descriptive and indicates compatibility or technical dependency.
