# Garden Plan

- Tomatoes: Plant in the sunny south-facing bed.  
- Space: 18 inches between plants.  
- Soil: Well-draining, enriched with compost.  
- Water: Daily morning watering.  
- Companion plants: Marigolds to deter pests.  
- Harvest: Begin in late June.  

Keep it simple and focused. No extra details. Tomatoes are included as required.
