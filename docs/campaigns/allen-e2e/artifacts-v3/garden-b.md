# Garden Plan

- Tomatoes: Plant in the south-facing bed for maximum sun exposure.  
- Spacing: 18 inches apart to allow growth and airflow.  
- Soil: Well-draining, rich in compost.  
- Watering: Daily in summer, early morning.  
- Companion plants: Marigolds to deter pests.  
- Harvest: Begin in late June.  

Keep the plan simple and focused on key elements. Tomatoes are the centerpiece. No extra details. No decorative language. Just essentials.
