# Garden Plan

## Introduction
This is a brief garden plan for your convenience.

## Tomatoes
Tomatoes are a must-have in any garden. They are easy to grow and provide a delicious addition to salads and meals.

## Vegetables
In addition to tomatoes, consider including some vegetables like lettuce, carrots, and cucumbers. These will not only add variety to your garden but also provide a good source of nutrients.

## Flowers
Don't forget to include some flowers in your garden. They will not only add color and beauty but also attract pollinators and other beneficial insects.

## Conclusion
Remember, gardening is a fun and rewarding hobby. With a little planning and effort, you can create a beautiful and thriving garden.
