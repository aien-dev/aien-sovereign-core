# Harbour, Orchard, and Windmill

The harbour is a bustling hub of activity, with boats and ships coming and going from the water's edge. The harbour is a vital part of the local economy, providing a safe and efficient way to transport goods and people. The harbour is also a popular spot for recreation, with many people visiting to enjoy the views and take part in water sports.

The orchard, located just a short walk from the harbour, is a peaceful oasis in the midst of the bustling town. The orchard is home to a variety of fruit trees, including apples, pears, and plums. Visitors can stroll through the orchard, taking in the sights and sounds of nature, and enjoying the fresh air and exercise.

The windmill, situated on the outskirts of the town, is a historic landmark and a popular tourist attraction. The windmill has been in operation for centuries, and is still used today to grind grain and pump water. Visitors can take a tour of the windmill and learn about its history and significance.
