# Harbour, Orchard, and Windmill

The harbour is a bustling hub of activity, with boats and ships coming and going at all hours. The harbour master is responsible for ensuring the safe and efficient operation of the harbour, and is often seen patrolling the waters. Nearby, the orchard is a tranquil oasis, filled with the sweet scent of blooming flowers and the gentle rustle of leaves. The orchard is home to a variety of fruit trees, including apples, pears, and plums. In the distance, the windmill stands tall, its sails turning gently in the breeze. The windmill is a historic landmark, and is often visited by tourists and locals alike.
