# Harbour, Orchard, and Windmill

The harbour is a bustling hub of activity, with boats and ships coming and going at all hours. The harbour master is responsible for ensuring the safe and efficient operation of the harbour, and is often seen patrolling the waters. Nearby, the orchard is a tranquil oasis, with rows of trees providing shade and a haven for wildlife. The orchard is home to a variety of fruits and vegetables, including apples, pears, and berries.
