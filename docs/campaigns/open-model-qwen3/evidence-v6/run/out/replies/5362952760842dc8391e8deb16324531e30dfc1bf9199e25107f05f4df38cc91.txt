# Knitting a Simple Scarf

## Materials
- Two skeins of chunky wool
- Size 10 knitting needles

## Steps
1. Cast on twenty stitches.
2. Knit every row until the scarf is long enough.
3. Check the width against your shoulders.
4. Cast off loosely and weave in the ends.

## Care
- Hand wash in cool water
- Dry flat
