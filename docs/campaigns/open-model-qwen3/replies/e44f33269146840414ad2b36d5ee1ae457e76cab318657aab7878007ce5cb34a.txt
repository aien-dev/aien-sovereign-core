# Choir Summary

## Key Points  
Marta led the warm-up exercises with a focus on breath control and vocal alignment, which helped the group feel more confident in their opening lines.  
Owen provided valuable feedback on the rhythm of the second verse, pointing out areas where the tempo needed to be more consistent.  
Priya introduced a new dynamic shift in the bridge section, which added emotional depth and improved the overall flow of the piece.  
The choir demonstrated strong harmony during the chorus, especially when singing in unison with proper pitch alignment.  
Marta noted that several members struggled with the high notes in the final stanza, suggesting a targeted vocal warm-up for next week.  
Owen emphasized the importance of listening to each other during ensemble sections to maintain balance.  
Priya suggested incorporating a brief pause before the final chorus to allow the audience to absorb the emotional impact.  
The group showed improved coordination during the transitions between sections, thanks to clear cues from Marta.  
Owen observed that the lower voices were slightly out of sync during the second verse, which affected the overall blend.  
Priya recommended practicing the piece slowly with a metronome to build precision in timing.  
Marta highlighted that the choir’s energy was noticeably higher after the mid-rehearsal break.  
Owen pointed out that the dynamics in the instrumental interlude needed more variation to match the emotional arc.  
Priya suggested adding a subtle vibrato to the sustained notes in the final section to enhance expressiveness.
