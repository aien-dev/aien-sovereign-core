# Packing Carry-On Checklist

## Documents
- [ ] Passport
- [ ] Travel insurance documents
- [ ] Visa or entry permit
- [ ] Flight itinerary
- [ ] Hotel reservation confirmation
- [ ] Emergency contact information
- [ ] Credit cards and cash

## Clothes
- [ ] Comfortable shoes
- [ ] 2 sets of underwear
- [ ] 2 sets of socks
- [ ] 1 light jacket or sweater
- [ ] 1 pair of pants or shorts
- [ ] 1 set of casual clothes
- [ ] 1 set of formal clothes (if needed)

## Electronics
- [ ] Charger
- [ ] Power bank
- [ ] Headphones or earbuds
- [ ] Travel adapter
- [ ] Smartphone and charger cable
- [ ] Camera or GoPro (if applicable)
- [ ] Portable speaker or Bluetooth device
