# Adopting a Rescue Cat

We had talked about a cat for years before we finally visited the shelter.

The first cat we met hid under a blanket and watched us with huge green eyes.

## Bringing Her Home
The drive home was quiet except for one small, worried meow.

We set up a quiet room with food, water and a box she could hide in.

For three days she only came out at night.

- On the fourth morning she was sitting on the windowsill when we opened the door.

- Now she follows us from room to room and sleeps at the end of the bed.
