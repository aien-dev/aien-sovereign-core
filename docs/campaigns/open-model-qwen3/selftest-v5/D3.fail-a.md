# Savings Basics

A savings account is a safe place to keep money you do not need today.

## Interest
The bank pays you interest for keeping money with it.
The rate is shown as a yearly percentage.
Interest is usually added every month.

## Paying In
A depositor adds money by transfer, at a branch, or with a cheque.
Each payment in raises the amount in the account.
Many people set up a regular transfer on payday.

## Withdraw
When you need money, you withdraw it.
Last month I withdrew a little for a birthday present.
Some accounts limit how often you can take money out.

## Balance
Your balance is the money in the account right now.
Check it in the app or on your statement.
A growing balance means your saving habit is working.
