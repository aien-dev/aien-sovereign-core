# Saving vegetable seeds

Saving your own seeds costs nothing and keeps the varieties you like.
Choose the healthiest plants and let a few fruits ripen fully.

## Collecting

Wait until the pods or fruits are dry or fully ripe.
Shake the seeds into a paper bag or scoop them out and rinse them.

## Drying

Spread the seeds in a single layer on a plate or a screen.
Keep them out of direct sun and let them dry for about two weeks.
Seeds are dry enough when they snap instead of bending.

## Labelling

Write the name, the year and the place on every packet.
Use pencil, because ink can fade.
A date lets you test old seeds before you sow them.

## Storage

Put the packets in a sealed jar with a spoonful of dry rice.
Store the jar somewhere cool and dark, such as a cupboard.
Most vegetable seeds stay good for two or three years.
