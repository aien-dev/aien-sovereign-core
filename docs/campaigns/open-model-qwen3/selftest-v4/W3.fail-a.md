# Saving vegetable seeds

Saving your own seeds costs nothing and keeps the varieties you like.

## Collecting

Wait until the pods or fruits are ripe.
Pick the healthiest plants first.
Let a few fruits ripen fully on the plant.
Open pods over a clean bowl.
Shake the seeds into a paper bag.

## Drying

Spread the seeds in a single layer on a plate.
Turn them every day.
Keep them out of damp air.
Keep them out of direct sun for about two weeks.

## Labelling

Write the name and the year on every packet.
Use pencil, because ink can fade.

## Keeping them safe

Put the packets in a sealed jar somewhere cool and dark.
Most vegetable seeds stay good for two or three years.
