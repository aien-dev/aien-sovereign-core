Choir rehearsal, Tuesday evening, church hall
Attendance was good: fourteen singers turned up.
Marta led the warm-up and worked on breathing for ten minutes.
Owen arrived late because his bus was delayed.
The group started with the folk song Down by the River.
Priya pointed out that the altos keep rushing the second verse.
We slowed the tempo and clapped the rhythm together.
Marta suggested marking the breath points in everyone's sheet music.
Owen offered to print fresh copies of the score before Friday.
The tenors still struggle with the high note near the end.
Priya will book the hall for two extra rehearsals in March.
We agreed the spring concert should be on the second Saturday of April.
Tea and biscuits were provided by the hall committee.
The next rehearsal starts at seven thirty sharp.
Everyone should bring a pencil and a water bottle.
